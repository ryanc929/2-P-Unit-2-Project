Mosh History

  • In the Late 1970s, Moshing (originally called "slam dancing" or "slamming") started in Calafornia's hardcore punk scene, notably around Huntington and Long Beach
  • In 1980s–90s, Moshing spread to Washington, D.C., Boston, and New York, and it would eventually cross over into other genres like heavy metal, grunge, hip-hop, and electronic music.

Why You Should Visit the Moshpit
